# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and inclusive.
- Assume positive intent.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or hateful language.
- Personal attacks, insults, or trolling.
- Public or private abuse of any community member.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.
They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this policy.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, open a private issue and mark it as conduct-related.
Maintainers will review and respond as quickly as possible.
